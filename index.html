METHODOLOGY

How to read the monitor

The tool is designed for investment research, not as a substitute for legal review. It distinguishes hard legal facts from an internal monitoring-priority indicator.

Monitoring priority

  • Very High: statewide regulation plus material local overlays or multiple restrictive mechanics in major markets.
  • High: material statewide cap, significant local regime, or concentrated exposure requiring active monitoring.
  • Moderate / Low: lighter current constraints or narrower local relevance after primary-source review.
  • Pending: no current conclusion; baseline audit not yet complete.

What is tracked

  • Statewide rent cap / stabilization formula
  • Vacancy reset, vacancy control and decontrol mechanics
  • Local rent-control authority or state preemption
  • New-construction and ownership exemptions
  • Good-cause / just-cause rules where economically relevant
  • Enforcement, audit, litigation and ballot / legislative changes
