Methodology

What the colors and feeds actually mean

Three separate layers

  • Baseline: current legal posture — statewide cap, local preemption/authority and priority local regimes.
  • Verified archive: developments tied to official/primary sources and retained chronologically.
  • Live 15-day discovery: automated web/news candidates. These are never allowed to silently rewrite the baseline.

Why green states still have feeds

  • Green means low current direct rent-amount constraint, not “no housing regulation.”
  • A preempted state can still have tenant protections, fee rules, registries, litigation or a bill to repeal preemption.
  • A state with no express preemption can move quickly at the local level; the activity layer is designed to catch that movement.
