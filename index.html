How to read the monitor

The monitor deliberately separates current law, property applicability, verified developments and automated discovery. That separation is what keeps the live layer useful without letting noisy headlines become underwriting facts.

Four separate layers

  • Curated legal baseline: current state posture and material local regimes. Priority jurisdictions are deep-dived against primary sources; the national layer is a screening baseline. This drives the base map.
  • Property coverage: the units/buildings actually subject to a rule, including new-construction/building-age tests and other exemptions.
  • Verified chronology: high-signal developments tied to official or primary sources.
  • 30-day discovery: U.S.-only automated monitoring candidates. This drives the momentum overlay, not the legal baseline.

Why two map layers?

  • A state can have no statewide cap while local or legislative activity is rising.
  • A headline about a proposal should not repaint a state as though the proposal were enacted law.
  • The momentum layer therefore shows Restrictive / Easing / Mixed / Quiet separately from the current legal structure.

Property coverage is an underwriting field

QuestionWhy it matters
What property type is covered?A rent cap that excludes a portfolio's asset class may have little direct NOI impact.
What is the building-age / new-construction test?California and Oregon use rolling 15-year tests at the statewide layer; Washington uses 12 years or less. Local ordinances can use different vintage cutoffs.
What other exemptions apply?Ownership, affordability programs, owner occupancy, unit count, tax benefits and tenancy type can change coverage.
What happens at vacancy?Vacancy control, partial decontrol and market reset create very different mark-to-market economics.
Research tool — not legal advice. Property coverage is fact-specific. Before an investment decision, verify the controlling statute/ordinance, effective date, ownership facts, unit count, certificate-of-occupancy date and tenancy status.
